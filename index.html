About PixelVault Cloud

A managed image hosting service for businesses that want less cloud complexity.

We provide managed image storage and delivery for businesses that need reliable hosting without directly maintaining object storage, CDN rules, access policies, and migration tasks. Our service is suitable for ecommerce stores, SaaS apps, agencies, media libraries, and custom websites.

What is included?

Managed S3-compatible bucket setup, image hosting configuration, public delivery support, migration guidance, monthly support, and clear subscription-based billing.

Clear service, billing, and refund policy

These details help customers understand what they are purchasing before checkout.

Service Delivery

After payment, setup starts within 1–2 business days. Access details and configuration information are shared by email after onboarding.

Billing

Plans are billed monthly. Payments are securely processed by Paddle, which may appear as the merchant of record on customer receipts.

Refunds

Customers may request a refund within 14 days if setup has not been completed or if the service cannot be delivered as described. Abuse, excessive usage, or completed custom migration work may be non-refundable.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide an actual S3 bucket image service?

Yes. The service includes an S3-compatible image storage bucket setup and managed configuration for image hosting and delivery.

Can you migrate images from AWS S3?

Yes. We can guide and support migration from AWS S3 to a managed S3-compatible storage setup based on the selected plan.

Is this suitable for ecommerce product images?

Yes. It is designed for product images, galleries, static assets, uploaded media, and public website image delivery.

Who owns the uploaded images?

The customer remains responsible for and retains rights to their uploaded content. Customers must only upload content they own or are legally allowed to host.
